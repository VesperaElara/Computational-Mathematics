## 2. Gauss-Jordan Method

### High-Level Overview
The **Gauss-Jordan Method** is a direct extension and modification of the standard Gauss-Elimination method. While Gauss-Elimination stops once the matrix is transformed into an upper-triangular form (requiring back-substitution), the Gauss-Jordan method uses elementary row operations to reduce the augmented matrix all the way into a **diagonal matrix** (specifically, an identity matrix on the left side). 

In this diagonalized form, each equation directly involves only a single unknown, meaning the values for variables like $x, y, z$ (and $w$) can be read straight off the final augmented column without needing any back-substitution steps.

---

### Step-by-Step Procedure
1. **Form the Augmented Matrix:** Write the given system of linear equations in matrix form $AX = B$, and combine them into the augmented matrix $[A \mid B]$.
2. **Forward Elimination (Making Zeros Below the Diagonal):** Use elementary row operations to create zeros below the main diagonal, similar to the initial steps of Gauss elimination.
3. **Diagonalization (Making Zeros Above the Diagonal):** Continue applying row operations to eliminate non-zero elements *above* the main diagonal, ensuring the coefficient matrix on the left transforms into an identity matrix ($I$).
4. **Read the Solution:** Once the left side is reduced to the identity matrix, the right-hand column directly gives the values of the unknowns.

---

### Worked Examples

#### Example 1: Solving a $3 \times 3$ System
Solve the system of equations using the Gauss-Jordan method:
$$x + y + z = 9$$
$$2x - 3y + 4z = 13$$
$$3x + 4y + 5z = 40$$

**Step 1: Write the Augmented Matrix**
$$[A \mid B] = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 & 1 & : & 9 \\ 2 & -3 & 4 & : & 13 \\ 3 & 4 & 5 & : & 40 \end{bmatrix}$$

**Step 2: Apply Row Operations to Create Zeros in the First Column**
Perform $R_2 \to R_2 - 2R_1$ and $R_3 \to R_3 - 3R_1$:
$$\sim \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 & 1 & : & 9 \\ 0 & -5 & 2 & : & -5 \\ 0 & 1 & 2 & : & 13 \end{bmatrix}$$

**Step 3: Normalize the Pivot in Row 2 and Eliminate**
Perform $R_2 \to -\frac{1}{5}R_2$:
$$\sim \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 & 1 & : & 9 \\ 0 & 1 & -2/5 & : & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 2 & : & 13 \end{bmatrix}$$

Perform $R_3 \to R_3 - R_2$:
$$\sim \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 & 1 & : & 9 \\ 0 & 1 & -2/5 & : & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 12/5 & : & 12 \end{bmatrix}$$

**Step 4: Scale Row 3 to Get a Leading 1**
Perform $R_3 \to \frac{5}{12}R_3$:
$$\sim \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 & 1 & : & 9 \\ 0 & 1 & -2/5 & : & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & : & 5 \end{bmatrix}$$

**Step 5: Back-Elimination (Creating Zeros Above the Diagonal)**
Eliminate the entry above in row 2 using $R_2 \to R_2 + \frac{2}{5}R_3$:
$$\sim \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 & 1 & : & 9 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 & : & 3 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & : & 5 \end{bmatrix}$$

Eliminate entries in row 1 using $R_1 \to R_1 - R_2$:
$$\sim \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 1 & : & 6 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 & : & 3 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & : & 5 \end{bmatrix}$$

Finally, eliminate the remaining non-zero entry in row 1 using $R_1 \to R_1 - R_3$:
$$\sim \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 & : & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 & : & 3 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & : & 5 \end{bmatrix}$$

**Final Answer:**
$$x = 1, \quad y = 3, \quad z = 5$$

#### Example 2: Another $3 \times 3$ System
Solve the system of equations using the Gauss-Jordan method:
$$x + y + z = 3$$
$$2x + 3y + z = 6$$
$$x - y - z = -3$$

**Step 1: Write the Augmented Matrix**
$$[A \mid B] = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 & 1 & : & 3 \\ 2 & 3 & 1 & : & 6 \\ 1 & -1 & -1 & : & -3 \end{bmatrix}$$

**Step 2: Eliminate Below the First Pivot**
Perform $R_2 \to R_2 - 2R_1$ and $R_3 \to R_3 - R_1$:
$$\sim \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 & 1 & : & 3 \\ 0 & 1 & -1 & : & 0 \\ 0 & -2 & -2 & : & -6 \end{bmatrix}$$

**Step 3: Continue Row Operations**
Perform $R_1 \to R_1 - R_2$ and $R_3 \to R_3 + 2R_2$:
$$\sim \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 2 & : & 3 \\ 0 & 1 & -1 & : & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & -4 & : & -6 \end{bmatrix}$$

**Step 4: Scale Row 3**
Perform $R_3 \to -\frac{1}{4}R_3$:
$$\sim \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 2 & : & 3 \\ 0 & 1 & -1 & : & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & : & 3/2 \end{bmatrix}$$

**Step 5: Complete Diagonalization**
Perform $R_1 \to R_1 - 2R_3$ and $R_2 \to R_2 + R_3$:
$$\sim \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 & : & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 & : & 3/2 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & : & 3/2 \end{bmatrix}$$

**Final Answer:**
$$x = 0, \quad y = \frac{3}{2}, \quad z = \frac{3}{2}$$

#### Example 3: A $4 \times 4$ System
Solve the system of equations using the Gauss-Jordan method:
$$2x - 2y = 1$$
$$-x + 2y - 3z = -2$$
$$-2y + 2z - 4w = -1$$
$$z - w = -3$$

**Step 1: Write the Augmented Matrix**
$$[A \mid B] = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & -2 & 0 & 0 & : & 1 \\ -1 & 2 & -3 & 0 & : & -2 \\ 0 & -2 & 2 & -4 & : & -1 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & -1 & : & -3 \end{bmatrix}$$

**Step 2: Normalize Row 1 Pivot**
Perform $R_1 \to \frac{1}{2}R_1$:
$$\sim \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -1 & 0 & 0 & : & 1/2 \\ -1 & 2 & -3 & 0 & : & -2 \\ 0 & -2 & 2 & -4 & : & -1 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & -1 & : & -3 \end{bmatrix}$$

**Step 3: Systematic Elimination across Rows**
Perform $R_2 \to R_2 + R_1$:
$$\sim \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -1 & 0 & 0 & : & 1/2 \\ 0 & 1 & -3 & 0 & : & -3/2 \\ 0 & -2 & 2 & -4 & : & -1 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & -1 & : & -3 \end{bmatrix}$$

Perform $R_3 \to R_3 + 2R_2$:
$$\sim \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -1 & 0 & 0 & : & 1/2 \\ 0 & 1 & -3 & 0 & : & -3/2 \\ 0 & 0 & -4 & -4 & : & -4 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & -1 & : & -3 \end{bmatrix}$$

Scale Row 3 using $R_3 \to -\frac{1}{4}R_3$:
$$\sim \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -1 & 0 & 0 & : & 1/2 \\ 0 & 1 & -3 & 0 & : & -3/2 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & 1 & : & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & -1 & : & -3 \end{bmatrix}$$

Perform $R_4 \to R_4 - R_3$:
$$\sim \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -1 & 0 & 0 & : & 1/2 \\ 0 & 1 & -3 & 0 & : & -3/2 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & 1 & : & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & -2 & : & -4 \end{bmatrix}$$

Scale Row 4 using $R_4 \to -\frac{1}{2}R_4$:
$$\sim \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -1 & 0 & 0 & : & 1/2 \\ 0 & 1 & -3 & 0 & : & -3/2 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & 1 & : & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 1 & : & 2 \end{bmatrix}$$

**Step 4: Backward Elimination to Form Identity Matrix**
Perform $R_3 \to R_3 - R_4$:
$$\sim \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -1 & 0 & 0 & : & 1/2 \\ 0 & 1 & -3 & 0 & : & -3/2 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & 0 & : & -1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 1 & : & 2 \end{bmatrix}$$

Perform $R_2 \to R_2 + 3R_3$:
$$\sim \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -1 & 0 & 0 & : & 1/2 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 & 0 & : & -9/2 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & 0 & : & -1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 1 & : & 2 \end{bmatrix}$$

Perform $R_1 \to R_1 + R_2$:
$$\sim \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 & 0 & : & -4 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 & 0 & : & -9/2 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & 0 & : & -1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 1 & : & 2 \end{bmatrix}$$

**Final Answer:**
$$x = -4, \quad y = -\frac{9}{2}, \quad z = -1, \quad w = 2$$
